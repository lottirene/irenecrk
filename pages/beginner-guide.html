Beginner Guide

Learn everything you need to know about CRK, and some tips to help you progress!

Getting Started

Rerolling

When you first start you may think... Should I reroll my account to try and get the best cookies?
In short, no rerolling is not needed in CRK. The game is very generous with its rewards, especially in the early stages. You should unlock all the cookies by the time you have done most of the Crispia Story. Due to powercreep, the Crispia story can be easily cleared by using whatevercookies you have, even just epic cookies.

Early Game Progression

In the early stages of the game, your main focus should be on completing the Crispia Story. In Crispia, you unlock toppings, and topping pieces, this is your first taste of how to upgrade your cookies to make them stronger, at the start, don't worry too much about toppings, or who needs what, as during the early game (especially PvE), toppings don't matter too much.

I'd recommend getting as far as possible, however at least 8-30 to unlock Beast Yeast, which will be very important for powering up your cookies, as that is where you can get Beascuits.

Upgrading Your Cookies

As you progress, there are more and more ways to upgrade your cookies, some of these are more complicated than others, but I'll go over them all!


Levelling Up

Using Exp jellies you can level up your cookies, exp jellies may seem hard to come by at first but there is one main way to get them, and that is the Strawberry Cake Tower. This tower provides enough EXP to level your main starter cookies to max level, and then you can put the rest of your cookies in the Hall of Ancient Heroes, to automatically level them to max.

The Hall of Ancient Heroes is unlocked by completing 3 seperate things:
> Clear Crispia 3-25
> Have 6 cookies at level 30 or higher
> Complete the Quest line given by the Sugar Maestro

Once you have the hall, you can place in your 5 highest levelled cookies, these are your Paragons. Every other cookie you place in the hall will automatically be levelled to the level of your lowest level paragon. So, if you have 5 level 100s, your cookies will all automatically be levelled to 100 once you place them in the Hall.

Skill Levels

The next main levelling method you will encounter is Skill Levelup via skill powders. During your early quests, (as well as clearing beast-yeast quests), you will recieve skill powders. You can also get skill powders from bounties everyday.
Skill powders can feel hard to come by but the best method in my opinion is:
> Clear the daily bounties, you can change the bounty setting to select only one type of powder, so you can focus on specific classes instead of getting random powders.
> Complete quests that give you skill powders, this includes the beginner quests, beast yeast quests, as well as any events which may be ongoing.
> You can also place cookies in the hall to max out skills. HOWEVER, you need FIVE of every class to do this, it goes based on your 5th highest skill level of each class.

Toppings

Toppings will be the first thing you encounter in game, you receive them randomly as a reward for completing Crispia stages, there are 10 different topping types, all of which provide a different stats% for your cookies. Toppings also contain substats, which can be either the same stat as the topping you're using or different ones.

When upgrading toppings you want to do the following things:
> Only upgrade EPIC toppings, these are the toppings which have a red background, and can have 3 different substats.
> Upgrade your toppings to level 6 first, check to see if the substats are good, if they are, upgrade to 9 and repeat, if not don't continue upgrading as this is a waste of resources.
> Topping pieces are a pain to come by, so you don't want to be wasting them!!

The best way to get toppings is from the Guild Gacha, this is where most people get all their toppings from. You can also get them from Crispia, the Balloon, or from events.

Beascuits

Beascuits are unlocked when you reach 8-30 Crispia, and unlock Beast Yeast. When upgrading Beascuits, I'd only recommend investing in Legendary Beascuits, because they have 4 stat lines, whereas the Epic only have 3, and the rare 2.
> To quickly get legendary beascuits, go to World 3, clear the boss at 3-5, and hire a 6* Dark Enchantress (or any other high rarity cookie), and just let that cookie solo the stage for you on Hard Mode. That way you can get a legendary beascuit of every type by just timeskipping the stage without worrying about dying.
Beascuits may seem confusing, but really they are simple. There are 3 types:

Regular
Regular beascuits are beascuits where you can roll all 4 lines into any stat, (except elemental stats), these beascuits are the most versatile, and probably what you're going to want to get to start off with.

Tainted
Tainted beascuits are beascuits with 4 lines, but you can only roll 3 of them, the top line is locked to a 20% elemental dmg stat, of a specific type (depending on the tainted beascuit type). These beascuits are used for niche uses, (guild battle, or seasonal modes), they aren't really necessary, and unfortunately, and only be obtained from old events no longer available, (but they will probably come back in the future).

Elemental
Elemental beascuits are beascuits where you can roll any stat, but in addition you can roll one specific elemental dmg stat also. These beascuits are used, most often in Guild Battle, or seasonal modes, but can be good for PvE as well. They're obtained from specific beast-yeast worlds depending which type you're looking for.

When resetting beascuits, there are two currencies used:
> Regular Dough, this dough is the most common, you usually get it from breaking down beascuits, it is used to reset all 4 lines at once.
> Radiant Dough (rads), this dough is much rarer, and is used to reset each line individually, you get it from breaking down beascuits, but usually the best way to get it is from events, specifically event shops.
When resetting beascuits, so you don't waste your rads, you want to roll using regular beascuits until you get two high substats of the stat you want. Generally it is recommended to get at least two purple lines, however one gold/one purple is the best case scenario (unless you're a god and natural roll 2 gold like a maniac). Once you have these two high level stats, that is when you can start rerolling your beascuit using rads.

Tarts

Tarts are another type of equipment and usually unlocked before beascuits, however are a little more time gated due to their rng nature. To get tarts, you need to unlock the mines, which can be done by clearing 3-25, and then clearing the nearby land, and completing the needed quests. To get tarts, you then pull on the tart gacha. You get the materials for the gacha from the mine venture and the ore mining, make sure you check your mines daily to have a healthy stream of gacha currency for the mines.

Kingdom Affairs

The kingdom side, (not necessarily the decorating your kingdom >;P), of the game is often neglected by new players, but is important to remember. You may not realise it, but your kingdom can give you many many buffs, which will help you along the way!

Landmarks

Landmarks are something you can buy, and upgrade, which buff all of your cookies stats. There are also some landmarks which give you useful rewards! The landmarks you should prioritise getting, and upgrading are:
> Morning Dew Garden (+10% HP)
> Dark Lord's Castle (+15% DMG Res)
> Chocolate Altar of the Fallen (+20% Crit Dmg)
> Dreaming Jelly Lion Statue (+10% Atk)
> Ominous Cake Tower (up to +60% HP)
> Eerie Haunted House (+8% Crit)

Sugar Gnome Lab

The Sugar Gnome Lab is one thing people ignore. Don't ignore it, it's there to help you and improve your existence! The Sugar Gnome lab may seem daunting, but really it is really simple!

There are two sections in the lab! Kingdom, and Cookie. The kingdom side is for upgrading your kingdom, mostly things like production speeds, the amount of exp you get from exp star jellies, easier good collection, faster house production, and of of the most important, the amount of coins you get from the tree of wishes!
To see the full Sugar Gnome Lab priority guide, see here!

Statue of Radiance

The statue of radiance is a statue which gives you additional effects such as increased atk, increased def, hp, coins and more!
To upgrade your statue of radiance, you're going to need one key thing, Radiant Shards. Radiant shards can be quite annoying to get, but here are the easiest ways to get them:
> Events, many events give out radiant shards, or you get them from the shops (The best example is Boss Rush Chest)
> The balloon dock, sending balloons daily, (I'd recommend doing 3 beast yeast ballons a day, then use worlds 7 or 8 to get good radiant shard/time), will steadily increase your radiant shard amount
> The various shops in game also give you (small) amounts, such as the arena shop, the seaside trade market, and guild shop!
> The fountain gives you a steady amount passively, just don't forget to claim your fountain rewards every 12 hours!

Production (aka the get rich quick scheme)

Production, everyone (read, me) hates production. But production, in reality, is your best friend! Production may seem useless, until you remember, the tree of wishes.
The tree of wishes is simply really, cookies want you to give them materials or goods, and in return they give you coins~! The cookies are kind of greedy, and always want so many goods, there's no end! So, you should aim to do production everyday, and in a (semi) efficient manner.
Production is a long game, but once you get it sorted, and keep restarting it, (pressing 4 buttons like twice a day), you will basically be unstoppable, and never run out of production materials and goods!
For a full indepth production guide, see here!

The gacha system

Best Cookies

Cookies has various uses, and almost every cookie is used for something. Where does your favourite cookie lie on the usability scale?
Check the various tierlists and find out~!

Mileage

Mileage is received from pulling on the gacha, whether this be the regular cookie gacha, the guild gacha, or the treasure gacha. (Not the costume gacha though, as this has its own form of currency as a reward from pulling it). Mileage can then be used to buy things from the mileage shop. But what should you use your mileage on?
Epic soulstones. This should be what you focus on first, now if you haven’t somehow unlocked all of the different epic cookies yet, you should buy the remaining soulstones for the ones you are missing. Next you should focus on the top epic cookies, and used in guild battle should be promoted to 5*, as well as some key PvE cookies. See the epic cookie tierlist to see which epic cookies you should unlock/promo to 5* first!
Next you should focus on treasure tickets, treasure tickets are going to be your best friend!! Pulling on the treasure gacha with crystals is really bad economically in game because you get more from pulling on the regular gacha and then buying treasure tickets with mileage instead! For The treasure tierlist see here!
The next thing I’d recommend spending your mileage on is time skips, these are going to save you when you need to do things like upgrade buildings, upgrade your labs, skip wishes you can’t do, upgrade your labs, skip the mines, upgrade your labs... PLEASE UPGRADE YOUR LABS!!!!
Lastly, please don’t spend your mileage on cubes, this is actually the worst investment. You’d probably get more from gambling on the ores in the mine for cubes than by wasting all your mileage :>

Game Modes

Story Mode

Story mode is pretty straight forward, you progress through and it tells you the plot, it’s pretty good and all voice acted. Or if you’re like me you can just press the skip button to get your crystals asap…
There’s two different types, crispia, and beast yeast, they both give good rewards, and you should do both. You’re going to have to do some of Crispia first because you need to beat 8-30 before you can unlock beast yeast.
Within Crispia, there’s 3 modes, story, dark, and master. They increase in difficulty as you go up, but to be honest they’re all pretty easy. It’s more Beast Yeast Hard you’re probably going to have issues with.

Bounties

Bounties are another “gamemode” really; these are a resource gaining mode, you should really only use bounties for 3 things.
Skill powders until you have 5 of each class to max lvl(100)
Cakes for the (name) HP landmark. There are only 3 ways to get these cakes, and only 2 permanent ways to get them, the trays, the bounties, and the rainbow shell gallery. So don’t neglect this bounty~!
Finally, you should use bounties on Sugar Crystals, to be honest this may be a little overkill, because if you’re consistently doing the mines daily you will be getting quite a few of the sugar crystal chests. But as a newer player trying to make magic candies and crystal jams you may feel the need to use your bounties on them too!
DO NOT use your bounties on coins. If you’re struggling for coins it is because your production is bad. Also don’t use them on experience either you get loads of it from the trays.

Trays

The trays are simply the best gift that the devs have ever given us. They’re so good, and at this point you can pretty much cheese them all with a double healer / Dark Enchantress combo.
Simply put, the trays are amazing because they give you loads of exp, coins, and other goodies, and they’re really easy even for beginner players. It auto levels both your cookie and their skill to lv70, and it ignores topping substats meaning you can just throw on any upgraded topping without having to worry about minmaxing~ truly incredible. Don’t sleep on them. At the moment, the team I’d recommend for brain dead clearing is:
Dark Enchantress, Awakened White Lily, Eternal Sugar, Sugar Swan, Millennial Tree.

Guild

From anti min max, to pro min max...
Guild is going to be either your best friend or your greatest enemy, for me it depends which side of the bed I wake up on…
Guild though, all jokes aside, is really amazing. I’d recommend joining the cookie run discord, (or you should join Himemori, we love beginners) and find an active guild. Joining an active guild gives you more rewards, and to be honest a sense of community. It’s a great way to make friends, and trust me CRK is much more fun when you can play with, and bully, your friends!

Arena

Arena is a mode again, you either love or you hate. Arena is like a PvP mode where well, your cookies fight other cookies! Arena teams change pretty regularly, usually every season (monthly). And you can guarantee someone has cooked some deranged comp which seems to counter everything. That’s just how the economy of arena works!
Arena rewards are pretty peak, do as best as you can, at the minute the most used cookies are:
Dark Enchantress, Millennial Tree, Eternal Sugar, Venom Dough, and Sugar Swan.
Treasures vary by comp, I’m not really an arena fanatic… but it’s still good to push for up to 30k crystals a month!

Alliance

Alliance is a nice mode, the team is usually the same every month because of electric supremacy… sometimes it changes a little but usually it’s swapping out a healer for a dmg dealer because the bosses do negative dmg…
Alliance gives you relics for your guild. You should donate to your guild because guild relics affect everyone’s stats in all the modes, such as more hp, more atk, etc!

Other general tips...

Burning Time

Every weekend there is a thing called Burning Time! This is a period where certain things either cost less or give you more items! You should use it wisely!! The biggest parts of burning time are:
The tree of wishes gives you 20% extra coins per wish, this seriously adds up, and from my experience, if you have the maxed out labs for the amount of coins the tree of wishes gives you then you actually profit from buying the items at the seaside market and doing the wishes with them!!
During burning time the extra rewards from mining in the mine gives you 100% more of the extra stuff, meaning you can get double the amount of topping pieces, juicy stamina jelly and more! The agar cubes melting time is also decreased so you can get the soulstones quicker!
You can also do beast yeast with 30% less juicy stamina jellies! This is perfect for farming beascuits or clearing the beast yeast story if you haven’t yet!

Bells

Bells are a useful little thing which can be as useless to giving you a 20% Atk Buff for Beast Yeast Episodes 6-8 where you don't need it. Or as useful to literally giving you double beascuit drops and making you rich!
Bells are given out via: GM2/1 Arena Ranking
Alliance GM1 finish, and random hero clears
Beast raids
